The Paradox of Participation: Organizing Authoritarianism in Democratic Brazil. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge Studies in Contentious Politics. Full manuscript submitted.

Civic participation strengthens democracy only when members govern the organizations they join and where those organizations' claims include all their fellow citizens—and the same organizational capacities can otherwise build authoritarian power. Based on a decade of fieldwork in Brazil, the book explains how the same civil society that helped democratize Brazil also helped upend it.

Full manuscript available upon request.
